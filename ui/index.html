Brokerage (Robinhood)

Connect your brokerage to sync your real holdings into the briefing. The connection is read-only — this app can never place trades — and your brokerage password is never stored here. You log in once through SnapTrade (which handles 2-step verification), and daily briefings sync automatically after that.

For now you bring your own free SnapTrade keys (a one-time, ~3-minute setup). This keeps your connection private to your own computer. A simpler one-click option may come later.

How to get your free SnapTrade keys
  1. Create a free account at dashboard.snaptrade.com.
  2. Create an app/project in the dashboard.
  3. Copy the Client ID and Consumer Key it gives you.
  4. Paste them below and click Save keys, then Connect Robinhood.
